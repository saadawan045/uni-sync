Privacy policy

Last updated 22 November 2026

uni-sync is an open-source tool that puts a student's timetable, deadlines and class updates into a Google Calendar. It runs on the student's own laptop, and the phone app runs in the student's own browser. There is no uni-sync server, and the maintainer never receives your data.

What uni-sync can access in your Google account

uni-sync asks Google for one permission, calendar.app.created. With it, uni-sync can create a calendar called "University" and read and change events in calendars it created. It cannot see your other calendars, your email, your contacts or anything else in your Google account.

Where your data goes

  • Laptop: your sign-in token and settings stay in files on your own laptop. uni-sync sends calendar changes straight to Google.
  • Phone app: the app talks to Google directly from your browser. Your sign-in token and a copy of your schedule (so it works offline) are kept in your browser's storage on that device.
  • uni-sync does not use analytics, ads or tracking, and does not sell or share any data.
  • Messages that uni-sync reads to find quizzes and deadlines are processed by an AI model running on your own laptop and are not sent anywhere else.

Google user data

uni-sync's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google data is used only to show and update your University calendar.

Removing access

Sign out in the phone app, or remove uni-sync at myaccount.google.com/permissions. You can delete the "University" calendar in Google Calendar at any time.
